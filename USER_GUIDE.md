## 📋 **Overview**

The MA Deal Room template system automatically creates transaction tasks based on property type. Each transaction gets:

- **125 universal tasks** (common to all MA real estate transactions)
- **24-31 property-specific tasks** (based on property type)
- **Total: 149-156 tasks per transaction**

All tasks include:
- Clear priorities (critical, high, normal, low)
- Due dates based on transaction milestones
- Legal citations for compliance tasks
- Time estimates for key activities
- Automatic reminders

---

## 🎯 **Understanding Task Priorities**

Tasks are prioritized to help you focus on what matters most:

### **Critical Priority** 🔴 (21 tasks)
**What it means:** Legal requirements, closing blockers, statutory deadlines

**Examples:**
- Lead paint disclosure (federal penalties up to $11,000)
- Title 5 septic certificate (required for closing)
- Smoke/CO detector certificate (mandatory at closing)
- Clear to close confirmation (lender requirement)

**Action Required:** Complete immediately, no delays tolerated

---

### **High Priority** 🟡 (76 tasks)
**What it means:** Important deadlines, contingency periods, financing milestones

**Examples:**
- Loan commitment deadline (30-45 days after P&S)
- Home inspection response (typically 3-5 days)
- Appraisal value check (affects financing)
- P&S signing deadline (5-14 days after offer)

**Action Required:** Complete on time to avoid contract issues

---

### **Normal Priority** 🟢 (104 tasks)
**What it means:** Standard transaction workflow tasks

**Examples:**
- Document collection
- Status updates
- Routine preparations
- Standard communications

**Action Required:** Complete in normal workflow sequence

---

### **Low Priority** 🔵 (13 tasks)
**What it means:** Post-closing follow-up, optional enhancements

**Examples:**
- Thank you notes
- Feedback requests
- Referral follow-up
- Resource sharing

**Action Required:** Complete when time permits

## 🏠 **Property Types**

The system supports 4 property types with specialized tasks:

### **Single-Family Home (Septic System)**
**Template:** `sfh_septic`
**Total Tasks:** 151 (125 base + 26 property-specific)

**Unique Tasks Include:**
- Title 5 septic inspection (required)
- Title 5 certificate (mandatory for closing)
- Well water comprehensive test
- Well flow test
- Septic pumping history
- Septic location diagram
- Well/septic distance verification

**Critical Compliance:**
- Title 5 inspection must be completed before closing
- Certificate valid for 2 years
- Failed system: 6-month post-sale repair option available

---

### **Single-Family Home (City Water)**
**Template:** `sfh_city_water`
**Total Tasks:** 149 (125 base + 24 property-specific)

**Unique Tasks Include:**
- Property survey review
- Roof inspection
- Basement water inspection
- Final water reading (Boston, Lynn, etc.)
- Various specialty inspections

**Critical Compliance:**
- Boston requires water final reading 2-3 weeks before closing
- Smoke/CO certificate required

---

### **Condominium**
**Template:** `condo`
**Total Tasks:** 153 (125 base + 28 property-specific)

**Unique Tasks Include:**
- Condo 6D certificate (required by M.G.L. c.183A §6)
- HOA financial review
- Condo questionnaire
- Master insurance review
- HOA meeting minutes
- Parking/storage verification
- Rental restrictions check

**Critical Compliance:**
- HOA must provide 6D certificate within 10 business days
- Review HOA financials carefully (special assessments, reserves)
- Verify rental restrictions before closing

---

### **Multifamily Property**
**Template:** `multifamily`
**Total Tasks:** 156 (125 base + 31 property-specific)

**Unique Tasks Include:**
- Tenant estoppel certificates
- Rent roll verification
- Operating expense analysis
- Lease audit review
- Security deposit transfer
- Tenant notifications
- Rental income verification

**Critical Compliance:**
- Tenant estoppel certificates required from all tenants

## 📅 **Task Timeline & Due Dates**

Tasks are scheduled relative to 4 key milestones:

### **Milestone 1: Listing**
When property enters MLS

**Tasks Due:**
- Property info in MLS (Day 0)
- Photography (Week 1)
- Agency disclosure (before showing)
- Lead paint disclosure prep (if pre-1978)

---

### **Milestone 2: Offer Accepted**
When offer is accepted by seller

**Tasks Due:**
- Collect earnest money (5 business days)
- Title 5 inspection (2-3 weeks)
- Home inspection (1-2 weeks)
- Lead paint disclosure (before P&S)

---

### **Milestone 3: P&S Signed**
When Purchase & Sale agreement executed

**Tasks Due:**
- P&S signing (5-14 days after offer)
- Loan commitment (30-45 days after P&S)
- Appraisal (2-3 weeks after P&S)
- Home inspection response (3-5 days after report)

---

### **Milestone 4: Closing**
When ownership transfers

**Tasks Due (Before Closing):**
- Clear to close (3-5 days before)
- Final walkthrough (24-48 hours before)
- Property vacant/clean (1 day before)
- Smoke/CO certificate (valid 60 days)
- Title 5 certificate (if septic)
- Municipal lien certificate (21 days before)

## ⚖️ **Legal Citations & Compliance**

Critical tasks include citations to MA laws and regulations:

### **Federal Regulations**
- **Lead Paint Disclosure** → 40 CFR 745 (EPA)
  - Required for homes built before 1978
  - Penalties up to $11,000 per violation
  - Must provide EPA pamphlet

### **MA General Laws (M.G.L.)**

**Chapter 111 - Lead Paint**
- Property Transfer Lead Paint Notification
- 105 CMR 460.720

**Chapter 148 - Fire Safety**
- Section 26F - Smoke Detectors (pre-1975 homes)
- Section 26F½ - Carbon Monoxide Alarms (all homes)

**Chapter 183A - Condominiums**
- Section 6 - Resale Certificate (6D Certificate)
  - HOA has 10 business days to provide
  - Must include financials, rules, meeting minutes

**Chapter 60, Section 23 - Municipal Liens**
- Municipality has 10 business days to issue
- Certificate valid 150 days when recorded

### **MA Regulations (CMR)**

**310 CMR 15.000 - Title 5 Septic**
- Inspection required for properties with septic
- Certificate valid 2 years from passing inspection
- Failed system: repair or 6-month post-sale option

## 🔄 **Common Workflows**

### **Workflow 1: Listing a Property**

**Week 1:**
1. Enter property in MLS (Day 1)
2. Schedule professional photography (Days 2-3)
3. Collect property documents from seller
4. Prepare agency disclosure

**Week 2:**
5. Activate listing
6. Schedule showings
7. Prepare lead paint disclosure (if applicable)

---

### **Workflow 2: Offer to P&S**

**Days 1-5 After Offer:**
1. Collect earnest money deposit (within 5 days)
2. Deposit EMD in escrow (within 1 day of receipt)
3. Order Title 5 inspection (if septic)
4. Schedule home inspection

**Days 5-14:**
5. Draft P&S agreement
6. Attorney review (3-5 days)
7. Negotiate terms (5-10 days)
8. Execute P&S (typically within 14 days)

---

### **Workflow 3: P&S to Closing**

**Weeks 1-2:**
1. Loan application submitted
2. Appraisal ordered
3. Home inspection completed
4. Inspection response negotiated
5. Title search ordered

**Weeks 3-4:**
6. Loan commitment expected (3-4 weeks)
7. Appraisal completed
8. Title commitment received
9. Clear title issues (if any)

**Weeks 5-6:**
10. Order municipal lien certificate (3 weeks before)
11. Schedule smoke/CO inspection (3 weeks before)
12. Order final water reading (if applicable)
13. Obtain Title 5 certificate (if septic)

**Final Week:**
14. Clear to close confirmed (3-5 days before)
15. Final document review with attorney
16. Schedule final walkthrough (24-48 hours before)
17. Property vacant and clean (1 day before)
18. Final walkthrough completed
19. **CLOSING DAY**

---

### **Workflow 4: Post-Closing**

**Week 1:**
1. Update MLS to sold status
2. Transfer keys and documents
3. Follow up with clients

**Weeks 2-4:**
4. Request client feedback
5. Request referrals
6. Share homeowner resources

## ❓ **FAQ**

### **Q: What if a task doesn't apply to my transaction?**
A: Tasks have applicability conditions. The system automatically hides tasks that don't apply based on property type, location, and other factors.

### **Q: Can I skip non-mandatory tasks?**
A: Yes. Tasks marked `mandatory: false` are optional. However, review them as they may still be beneficial.

### **Q: What if I miss a critical deadline?**
A: Critical tasks have multiple reminders. If you miss one:
- Consult with attorney immediately
- Check if extension is possible
- Understand potential consequences (penalties, contract issues)

### **Q: How do I handle failed Title 5 inspection?**
A: Options:
1. Repair system before closing (preferred)
2. Price adjustment to buyer
3. 6-month post-sale repair window (buyer assumes risk)

### **Q: When should I order municipal lien certificate?**
A: Order 3-4 weeks before closing. Municipality has 10 business days to issue, and you want buffer time.

### **Q: What if HOA doesn't provide 6D certificate on time?**
A: M.G.L. c.183A §6 requires 10 business days. If delayed:
- Send follow-up request in writing
- Consult attorney about options
- May need to adjust closing date

### **Q: Are the time estimates accurate?**
A: Estimates are typical timelines for MA transactions. Actual times may vary by:
- Municipality (processing times vary)
- Season (busy periods take longer)
- Vendor availability
- Complexity of issues

### **Q: Can I add custom tasks?**
A: Yes. Consult with your administrator to add transaction-specific tasks.

### **Q: How are priorities determined?**
A: Priorities are based on:
- Legal requirements (critical)
- Contractual deadlines (high)
- Standard workflow (normal)
- Nice-to-have activities (low)

### **Q: What if two tasks conflict?**
A: Follow priority order:
1. Critical tasks first (legal/compliance)
2. High priority tasks second (deadlines)
3. Normal workflow third
4. Low priority when time permits
